restore interval, RPO or RTO. The reviewed
[branch API](https://api-docs.neon.tech/reference/createprojectbranch) supports
`parent_id` with `parent_timestamp` or `parent_lsn`. A restricted later exercise
can create **a new recovery-validation child** from this explicit validation

minimum suspended compute and compare known synthetic states/security. Preserve
the original branch and all post-point writes. No recovery branch, snapshot,

whole branch timeline across all its databases; it is not a merge. Do not use it
as the first recovery experiment or assume it reconciles later writes. A branch
fork also copies its other databases and roles: isolate it from production and
revalidate credentials, ownership, grants, RLS and revoked sessions before access.
Scheduled/paid snapshot retention and exported archives need separate allowance,
storage/access and owner decisions. Supabase remains unavailable for recovery.
